Walk through the house

  • W A S D or arrow keys to walk, hold Shift to go faster
  • Click the view to look around with the mouse (Esc releases it). You can also drag to look.
  • Click a door or press E near it to open or close it
  • Use the joystick (bottom left) to walk
  • Drag anywhere else to look around
  • Tap a door, or the Open door button when you're close, to open or close it
  • Go up the stair behind the gym to reach the roof terrace
  • Rooms menu jumps you straight to any room
  • Overview switches to a bird's-eye 3D view (drag to orbit, pinch or scroll to zoom). Tap Walk to go back.
